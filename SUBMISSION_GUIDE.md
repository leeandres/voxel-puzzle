## 🎯 itch.io (Recomendada)

### Paso 1: Crear Cuenta

1. Ve a [itch.io](https://itch.io)
2. Haz clic en **"Register"**
3. Completa tu perfil de desarrollador

### Paso 2: Crear Nuevo Juego

1. Ve a **"Dashboard"** → **"Create New Project"**
2. Completa la información:

#### Información Básica

```
Title: The Forgotten Depths
Short description: A cooperative voxel puzzle adventure
```

#### Descripción Completa

```
⛏️ THE FORGOTTEN DEPTHS ⛏️

A cooperative voxel puzzle adventure where players explore ancient ruins, 
solve environmental puzzles, and discover hidden secrets.

🎮 FEATURES:
• Procedurally generated worlds with 4 unique biomes
• Environmental puzzles: mirrors, colors, weights, and keys
• Asymmetric co-op for 2-4 players
• Hidden secret rooms and treasures
• 50 journal pages with ancient lore
• Immersive procedural audio
• Day/night cycle
• Achievement system

🌍 BIOMES:
🌲 Petrified Forest - Ancient stone trees guard secrets
💎 Crystal Caves - Glowing crystals light the underground
🏛️ Sunken Temple - Ancient ruins hold forgotten knowledge
🕳️ The Abyss - Danger lurks in the deepest depths

🤝 COOPERATIVE ROLES:
🗺️ Cartographer - Sees the full map
🔍 Explorer - Moves and interacts
🔐 Cipher - Solves codes and riddles
👁️ Vigil - Detects dangers

⌨️ CONTROLS:
WASD - Move
Mouse - Look
E/Click - Interact
Space - Jump
Shift - Sprint
1-4 - Select tool
F - Flashlight
J - Journal

🆓 100% FREE - Play in your browser!
```

#### Tags

```
puzzle, adventure, cooperative, voxel, minecraft, 
exploration, secrets, browser, free, html5, 
multiplayer, indie, retro, pixel
```

#### Classification

```
Type: HTML
Genre: Adventure, Puzzle
Platform: Web
```

### Paso 3: Subir Archivos

1. Haz clic en **"Upload files"**
2. Sube todos los archivos de tu juego:

```
📁 the-forgotten-depths.zip
├── index.html
├── tutorial.html
├── dashboard.html
├── login.html
├── audio-system.js
├── audio-controls.html
└── NEW_PUZZLES_AND_SECRETS.js
```

3. Marca **"This file will be played in the browser"**

### Paso 4: Configurar Página

1. **Screenshot**: Sube 3-5 screenshots (1280x720 mínimo)
2. **Cover image**: 630x500 píxeles
3. **Background**: 1920x1080 píxeles (opcional)

### Paso 5: Configuración de Precio

1. Selecciona **"Free"**
2. Opcional: **"Name your own price"** (propinas)

### Paso 6: Publicar

1. Haz clic en **"Save & View"**
2. Verifica que todo se vea bien
3. Haz clic en **"Publish"**

## 🎯 Newgrounds

### Paso 1: Crear Cuenta

1. Ve a [newgrounds.com](https://newgrounds.com)
2. Regístrate como **"Developer"**

### Paso 2: Subir Juego

1. Ve a **"Portal"** → **"Submit"**
2. Selecciona **"Game"**

#### Información Requerida

```
Title: The Forgotten Depths
Description: [Usar la misma descripción de itch.io]
Tags: puzzle, adventure, cooperative, voxel, browser
Type: HTML5
```

### Paso 3: Subir Archivos

1. Sube tu juego como **ZIP**
2. Asegúrate de que `index.html` esté en la raíz

### Paso 4: Configurar Arte

```
Game icon: 150x150 píxeles
Screenshot 1: 640x480 (gameplay)
Screenshot 2: 640x480 (puzzle)
Screenshot 3: 640x480 (co-op)
```

### Paso 5: Enviar para Revisión

1. Haz clic en **"Submit for Review"**
2. Espera aprobación (1-3 días)
